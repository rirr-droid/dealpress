# DealPress Pricing Strategy - Optimized for $1K MRR

## Overview

The pricing has been redesigned to reach $1,000 MRR as quickly as possible by:
1. **Higher price points** - $49 and $99/month (vs. $10)
2. **Value-based tiers** - Priced on value delivered, not just features
3. **Restrictive free tier** - Creates urgency to upgrade
4. **Seat-based limits** - Natural expansion revenue

## Path to $1,000 MRR

With this pricing structure, you need far fewer customers:

| Scenario | Customers Needed | Mix |
|----------|-----------------|-----|
| All Professional ($49/mo) | **21 customers** | 21 × $49 = $1,029 |
| All Business ($99/mo) | **11 customers** | 11 × $99 = $1,089 |
| **Realistic Mix** | **~15 customers** | 10 Professional + 5 Business = $985 |

Compare to old pricing:
- $10/month = 100 customers needed ❌
- $49/month = 21 customers needed ✅
- $99/month = 11 customers needed ✅

## Pricing Tiers

### Starter - $0/month (Free)
**Purpose:** Lead generation and product qualification

**Limits:**
- 3 approval requests/month (very restrictive)
- 1 workflow template only
- 1 user
- Email notifications only
- 7-day data retention

**Why these limits work:**
- 3 requests/month = runs out quickly
- 1 template = can't test different workflows
- 1 user = can't collaborate
- Forces upgrade within first month of real usage

## Customer Journey & Conversion Funnels

### Funnel 1: Free → Professional (Primary)
**Trigger:** User hits 3 request limit or needs 2nd user

**Conversion tactics:**
1. Show "2/3 requests used" warning
2. Block request #4 with upgrade CTA
3. Email: "You're getting value, upgrade for more"
4. Offer: First month 20% off ($39)

**Expected conversion:** 15-20%

### Funnel 2: Professional → Business (Expansion)
**Trigger:** User approaching user limit or request limit

**Conversion tactics:**
1. Show "4/5 users added" warning
2. Analytics: "You're using 45/50 requests"
3. Email: Success story about analytics/SLA features
4. Offer: Upgrade now, credit remaining Professional days

**Expected conversion:** 25-30%

### Funnel 3: Business → Enterprise (White glove)
**Trigger:** Manual qualification, 15 user limit

**Sales tactics:**
1. Assign account exec when hitting 12+ users
2. Schedule ROI review call
3. Present custom package
4. Annual contract with discount

## Pricing Psychology

### Anchoring
- Business plan ($99) makes Professional ($49) feel like a deal
- Enterprise (custom) makes Business feel accessible
- Free tier makes people feel like they're winning

### Value Metrics
- Requests/month = usage-based (fair)
- Users = team size (scales with company)
- Features = value-based (analytics, SLA worth more)

### Friction Reduction
- No credit card for free tier
- Cancel anytime (no long contracts until Enterprise)
- Upgrade/downgrade instantly
- Proration handled automatically
